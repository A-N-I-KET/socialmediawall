# 🚀 Hackathon Project Submission Guide

Welcome hackers! This guide covers everything you need to successfully submit your hackathon project. You can copy this guide into Notion to share with participants or send it out in your communication channels.

---

## 1️⃣ Login & Account Setup
1. **Access the Portal**: Click on the submission portal link from the main hackathon website.
2. **Enter Email**: Start by entering your registered email address.
   > **Note**: You must be a registered participant. If your email isn't recognized, contact an organizer!
3. **First-Time Setup**:
   - If this is your first time logging in, you will be prompted to fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
   - Create a secure **Password** (min 6 characters).
4. **Returning Hackers**: Simply enter your password to continue working on your submission.

## 2️⃣ The Submission Form

Here is all the information you will need to provide for your project. Having these answers drafted ahead of time is highly recommended!

### Core Details
* **Project Name** *(Required)*: Keep it concise and catchy (e.g., *AI-Powered Study Assistant*).
* **Short Description** *(Required)*: A brief one-liner about your project. This has a strict limit of **200 characters**.

### Project URLs
* **Project Links**: Add URLs to your GitHub repository, live demo, YouTube pitch, presentation slides, or design files. You can add multiple links!

### Deep Dive (Markdown Supported!) ✍️
> **Pro Tip**: The following text boxes fully support **Markdown formatting**! Use headers, bold text, bulleted lists, and code blocks to make your submission look professional and easy to read.

* **Problem It Solves**: In detail, describe the exact problem your project targets. What can people use it for? How does it make existing tasks easier, faster, or safer?
* **Challenges I Ran Into**: Tell us the story behind the build. Did you encounter a nasty bug, a tricky API limitation, or a design challenge? How did your team pivot and overcome it?

### Tech Stack & Media
* **Technologies Used**: What did you build this with? Type a technology (e.g., *React, Firebase, Tailwind*) and press **Enter** to add it as a stylized badge to your project profile.
* **Project Images**: A picture is worth a thousand words! Upload up to **4 images or screenshots** showcasing your UI, architecture, or demo.
  * *Requirements*: Max **10MB** per image. Formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP.

## 3️⃣ Submitting & Editing

* **Ready to go?** Review your answers and hit the **Submit Project 🚀** button!
* **Making Changes**: Did you find a typo or want to add a fresh screenshot right before the deadline? Don't worry! As long as the hackathon submission window is still open, you can return to the portal at any time to click **Edit Your Submission ✏️** and update your details.
